How to run it at an event

  1. Open a scene on the projector or TV and press F for full screen.
  2. Kids draw a ship on white paper, pointing right →. Thick crayons or markers work best.
  3. Take a photo and press 📷 Upload, drag the photo onto the screen, or draw straight on a tablet with ✏️ Draw.
  4. The white paper is removed automatically and the ship flies in.

Keys: D draw · S surprise ship · F full screen. Add ?demo=0 to start with an empty sky, or ?ui=0 to hide the buttons.
